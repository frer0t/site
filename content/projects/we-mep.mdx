## Context

MEP work (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) lives on sites and in offices at the same time. Progress gets lost in WhatsApp threads, drawings live in someone’s drive, and service requests die in email. Timelines slip because nobody shares one source of truth.

**WE-MEP** is the mobile app where field and office stay on the same job.

## What WE-MEP does

- **Real-time progress tracking** across active MEP jobs — what’s done, what’s blocked, what’s next
- **Team communication** inside the app so updates don’t scatter across chats
- **Document management** for plans, site files, and references crews need on location
- **Service request handling** so issues become tracked work, not forgotten messages
- **Payments, SMS, and email** wired into the flow — confirmations and alerts without a separate ops stack

## Who it’s for

MEP crews and managers who need one place to answer “where are we?” — without three apps and a paper log.

## My role

Built the mobile app end to end, including payment, SMS, and email integrations so operational events reach the right people.

## Outcome

Production app at [we-mep.rw](https://we-mep.rw/app). Progress, documents, messaging, and payments feel like one system — not bolted-on extras.
