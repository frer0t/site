## Context

Reporting a service issue to government often means walking into an office, waiting, and hoping someone follows up. Citizens lose visibility the moment the form leaves their hands. Officials drown in unstructured complaints with no shared queue or status trail.

**Irekure** is the web platform that closes that loop — built for the Tech Associate Program by RISA, the Rwanda ICT Chamber, and Hanga Hubs.

## What Irekure does

- **Citizens** submit problems with enough context to act on, then track progress without calling anyone
- **Officials** triage incoming issues, assign ownership, and respond from a management view
- **Status history** so both sides see the same timeline — filed → in review → resolved

## Who it’s for

Citizens who need to report service issues, and government officials who need a clear queue to respond — without chasing each other through offices and side channels.

## How it works

1. A citizen files a complaint with category, location, and description
2. The issue lands in an official queue for triage
3. Updates and responses push back into the citizen’s tracking view
4. Accountability lives in the shared status trail

## Outcome

A working submit → track → respond loop. Open source, with a public demo and source on GitHub.
