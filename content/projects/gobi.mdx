## Context

A single shipment can involve shippers, forwarders, transporters, clearing agents, warehouses, and government processes. Each party may have its own tools — but coordination still falls back to WhatsApp, email, spreadsheets, PDFs, and calls.

**Gobi** is the operational coordination layer around a shipment. It connects people, organizations, and systems in one shared view — without replacing ERP, TMS, fleet tools, or government trade systems.

## What Gobi does

- **Shared shipment record** — details, parties, locations, and documents in one place
- **Documents & handoffs** — request, exchange, and confirm what each party owns
- **Milestones & exceptions** — follow each phase, surface blockers and delays
- **Proof & completion** — confirm delivery and keep a traceable execution record

## Who it’s for

Logistics operators coordinating work **between** organizations — trusted partners, system-agnostic, focused on what is happening, what is blocked, and what needs to happen next.

## How it works

1. **Create shipment** — bring the shipment into one operational record
2. **Coordinate work** — responsibilities, documents, handoffs, and milestones across parties
3. **Execute & resolve** — track progress, manage exceptions, confirm completion
