## Context

Healthcare providers juggle appointments, records, and patient follow-up across tools that weren’t designed to talk to each other. Every extra login is time stolen from care.

The **Eden Care Provider Portal** is the web app where that work lives in one place.

## What the portal does

- **Appointments** — schedule, manage, and follow through so care isn’t delayed by admin chaos
- **Patient care & records** — medical context and history available when the provider needs it
- **Provider–patient communication** — updates and follow-ups without scattering across side channels

## Who it’s for

Clinicians and care teams on the Eden Care network who need a single surface for day-to-day provider work — not a pile of disconnected tools.

## My role

Worked on the provider portal at Eden Care Medical across frontend and backend — UI for provider workflows and REST APIs that power appointments, records, and care operations.

## Outcome

Providers get one portal for care work instead of a scavenger hunt across apps. Less admin friction, clearer patient context, faster follow-through.
